# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository should remain a professional, constructive, and safe public space. Participants are expected to communicate in good faith, respect differing perspectives, and focus discussion on the profile, its public project references, and their maintenance.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, provide relevant context for proposed changes, and protect the privacy of clients, collaborators, and community members. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deceptive impersonation, and publication of confidential or personal information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a conduct concern, contact [alex@techtideai.io](mailto:alex@techtideai.io) with the subject line `Confidential: profile conduct report`. Reports will be handled privately to the extent practical. Do not disclose sensitive personal details in a public issue or pull request.
